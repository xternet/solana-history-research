## Funding versus vendor billing

Keep twelve months of hosting in the project budget, even if vendors invoice in
shorter periods. A staged grant release can reduce upfront exposure without
charging hosting again in the $5,000/month maintenance work fee.

Hosting and data transfers are needed before M2 delivery. The full R&D + Workers
fee is paid on technical acceptance.

The proposed M2 schedule is **$36,383 initially** ($35,383 Jan–Jun hosting and
$1,000 transfers), **$28,682 for R&D + Workers on technical acceptance**,
then **$34,935 for Jul–Dec hosting**, released after June review and before renewal
payments fall due. These sum to $100,000; the second hosting tranche is not an
extra charge in the maintenance budget. June review checks invoices, service,
capacity and the next term. Foundation approval and matching vendor terms are
required; do not sign a 24-month obligation against twelve funded months.

Alternatively, the Foundation may pay approved hosting invoices for either
tranche directly to providers, subject to agreement. These payments count toward
M2's existing budget and are deducted from funds transferred to the applicant;
the applicant does not receive or claim those hosting amounts again.
